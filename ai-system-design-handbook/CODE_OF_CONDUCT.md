# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome beginners, explain corrections without ridicule, and distinguish disagreement about a design from judgment about a person.

Do not harass others, publish personal or confidential information, spam discussions, or present another person's work as your own. Respect source licenses and access boundaries. Use synthetic examples when discussing customer situations.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request edits, or restrict participation to protect the learning community. Raise concerns through GitHub's reporting tools or an appropriate private channel when available; do not expose sensitive evidence in a public issue.
