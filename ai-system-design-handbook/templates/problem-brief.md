# Problem brief

**Project / date / owner:**

## User and decision

Who performs the task? What decision or action should become easier? Who owns the final outcome?

## Current process and evidence

Describe a recent difficult example. Record source systems, manual steps, exception types, volume, time per task, errors, and the measurement period. Label missing baseline information explicitly.

## Proposed first slice

Choose one user group, one task, one source, and one reviewable output. State allowed actions and explicit non-goals. Explain why AI is useful compared with a rule, SQL query, search page, or ordinary integration.

## Data and constraints

Record source ownership, required access, sensitivity, retention, processing locations, approved vendors, and dependencies. Define freshness, latency, quality, availability, cost, and accessibility requirements with units and owners.

## Acceptance criteria

Write observable pass conditions. Include normal, missing-information, denied-access, duplicate, and dependency-failure behavior. Define the dataset, rubric, sample size, and threshold used to assess quality.

## Pilot and stop conditions

Define cohort, review owner, duration, monitoring, rollback/disable path, and the result that warrants expansion. List open questions and the experiment or person that resolves each.
