# Acknowledgments and originality

[Om Bharatiya's AI System Design Guide](https://github.com/ombharatiya/ai-system-design-guide) inspired the idea of an open, navigable AI system-design learning repository. Credit belongs to its maintainer and contributors for that resource.

This project develops a separate learning sequence in plain English, with original explanations, worked fictional cases, exercises, and independently written offline Python labs. It does not rebrand or mirror the reference repository's chapters, code, branding, interview bank, or model/pricing tables. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Technical concepts belong to the broader research and engineering community. [RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md) links primary papers and official documentation. These links are for attribution and further study; externally linked material retains its own copyright and terms.

Contributors must identify any reused third-party material and preserve its required notices. Prefer a short original explanation and a source link over copying a long passage.
