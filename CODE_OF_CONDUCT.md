# Code of Conduct

We want a practical, respectful learning community. Be constructive, assume good intent, challenge ideas with evidence, and protect the privacy of customers and contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, unsafe deployment advice, fabricated results, and publication of confidential information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns privately through the contact method on [Swapnil AI Labs](https://swapnilailabs.netlify.app/). Do not open a public issue containing personal or confidential details.
