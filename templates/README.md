# Reusable Templates

Copy these files into a project and replace brackets. Delete sections that truly do not apply, but record why material controls are not applicable.

| Template | Decision it supports |
|---|---|
| [Problem brief](./problem-brief.md) | Is this problem worth solving, and what does success mean? |
| [Architecture decision record](./architecture-decision-record.md) | Why was this technical choice made? |
| [Evaluation plan](./evaluation-plan.md) | What evidence permits a release? |
| [Threat and data-control register](./threat-data-register.md) | Which risks exist and who owns controls? |
| [Pilot charter](./pilot-charter.md) | Who enters the pilot, and when do we stop or expand? |
| [Operations runbook](./runbook.md) | How does an operator detect and recover? |
| [ROI model](./roi-model.md) | Does measured value justify total cost? |
| [Outcome review](./outcome-review.md) | What changed, what did not, and what is the next decision? |

Keep facts, measurements, stakeholder claims, and assumptions visibly distinct. Dates, owners, evidence links, and expiry conditions turn templates into operating tools.
