# 11 — Commercial Thinking and Executive Communication

## Outcome

Connect system performance to credible business value and a clear customer decision.

## Learn

- Value pools: labor capacity, delay, errors, risk, revenue, and experience
- Baseline, counterfactual, attribution, confidence, and measurement windows
- Total cost: build, model, infrastructure, licenses, support, review, and change
- ROI, payback, unit economics, ranges, and sensitivity analysis
- Executive updates that surface decisions, evidence, risks, and asks
- Scope of work, milestones, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and exclusions
- Expansion based on a repeatable outcome rather than feature accumulation

## Build

Create an ROI model for the capstone with conservative, base, and upside scenarios. Separate measured inputs from customer-provided inputs and assumptions. Include adoption, exception handling, ongoing human review, and failure cost.

Write a one-page executive update covering: outcome, evidence, risk, decision needed, and next 30 days. Deliver a five-minute presentation followed by objections from finance, security, operations, and an end user.

## Evidence

- [ROI model](../templates/roi-model.md)
- Sensitivity analysis
- Scope and acceptance criteria
- Executive memo or recorded update
- Objection log and decisions

## Proof gate

The model cannot claim saved cash when it only creates theoretical capacity. Material assumptions are visible and adjustable. The executive update ends with a specific decision and names the accountable owner.
