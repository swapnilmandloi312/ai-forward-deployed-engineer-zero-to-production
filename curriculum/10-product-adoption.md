# 10 — Productization, Adoption, and Handoff

## Outcome

Run a bounded pilot that users can adopt, operators can support, and the customer can own after handoff.

## Learn

- Pilot cohort, entry criteria, success, stop, and expansion conditions
- Human-in-the-loop interface and correction capture
- Trust calibration, explainability, accessibility, and change management
- Adoption funnels: invited, activated, repeated, retained, and outcome achieved
- Support boundaries, ownership, training, documentation, and service levels
- Feedback collection that separates preference from task failure
- Field learning that becomes reusable product capability

## Build

Design a four-week pilot for 5–20 users. Define eligible cases, excluded high-risk cases, expected volume, business and safety metrics, support channel, daily review, and rollback authority.

Observe at least two users attempt a realistic task without coaching. Record friction and hidden work. Improve the workflow or interface, then measure the change. Prepare a handoff for the operating owner, including routine checks, known limitations, escalation, and change control.

## Evidence

- Pilot charter and RACI
- User journey and adoption funnel
- Observation notes and resulting changes
- Operator training and handoff checklist
- Go/no-go/expand recommendation

## Proof gate

The pilot has an owner, controlled cohort, explicit exclusions, stop condition, and decision date. A user can complete the core task and an operator can handle common failures without the builder present.
