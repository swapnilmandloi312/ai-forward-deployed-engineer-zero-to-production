## Calculation

For each row, multiply `weight × score ÷ 3`. Sum the results for a percentage out of 100.

| Readiness | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 0–39 | Build foundations and one narrow vertical slice |
| 40–59 | Project-ready; reliability and field evidence still thin |
| 60–74 | Interview-ready for scoped roles with a strong case study |
| 75–89 | Ready to own a pilot with senior review |
| 90–100 | Strong evidence across build, operate, and customer outcomes |

The total can hide critical risk. A score below 2 in evaluation, security, or incident response blocks “production-ready” regardless of the average.
